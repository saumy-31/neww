Have Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Get quick answers regarding admissions, scholarships, and batch timings.

What is the batch size at NEXUS ACADEMY?
We strictly limit our regular classroom batches to a maximum of 30-35 students to ensure individual personal attention and active interactive learning.
Do you offer scholarships for meritorious students?
Yes! Through our NEXUS Admission Test (NAT), deserving students can win up to 100% fee waivers based on their performance in Mathematics and Science.
What happens if a student misses a lecture?
Every lecture is recorded in high definition and uploaded to the NEXUS Student Mobile App within 24 hours for easy backup viewing and revision.
Are there separate doubt-clearing sessions?
Yes, dedicated 2-hour doubt counters are operated every daily after regular class hours where students can interact 1-on-1 with subject faculty.
