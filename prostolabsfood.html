Unfiltered Gastronomy

We stripped away fine-dining pretension to focus on pure flavor.

Founded by Chef Mateo Vance in 2024, ALMA was born out of a desire to connect people through raw, energetic dining. We harvest produce from our private 12-acre farm every morning and cook over open embers.

No white tablecloths. No stiff dress codes. Just vibrant plates, natural low-intervention wines, and good music.
