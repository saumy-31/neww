Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Clear answers to common questions about our procedures, pricing, and appointments.

How quickly can I see a doctor at Mediva?

Most virtual appointments can be booked same-day within 2 hours. In-clinic consultations are typically available within 24 to 48 hours depending on your chosen specialist.

Do you accept major health insurance plans?

Yes! We partner with over 40 primary insurance carriers. Additionally, we provide transparent out-of-pocket self-pay rates and detailed itemized receipts for reimbursement submission.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring a valid photo ID, your insurance card, and any relevant past medical records or medication lists. You can also upload these securely beforehand through our online portal.

How are diagnostic test results delivered to me?

All lab and imaging results are uploaded to your encrypted Mediva Patient Portal within 24 hours. Your attending physician will follow up with a summary call or video note.
